Format

{"surah": 67, "audio_url": "https://cdn.mp3quran.net/audio/muhammad-luhaidan/r1/067.mp3",
 "verse_timings": [{"verse_key": "67:1", "timestamp_from": …, "timestamp_to": …,
   "segments": [[1, start_ms, end_ms], [2, start_ms, end_ms], …]}, …]}

segments are [word position, start ms, end ms] in time order. The recordings are from prayer and the reciter sometimes repeats a phrase — then word positions appear again, exactly as in quran.com's own data. Word numbers are quran.com's, so the text comes from your own Qur'an text source.

How it was made

Tarteel's Qur'an model (whisper-base-ar-quran) finds each ayah; a Qur'an-trained wav2vec2 model finds every letter of the known mushaf text by forced alignment, so the data can never contain a wrong word or haraka. Repeats the model hides are re-heard and checked letter by letter against the audio; doubtful words are flagged (2.9 %). Details and accuracy measurements are in the README.

بالعربية

بيانات مجانية مفتوحة لتوقيت كل كلمة من كلمات القرآن الكريم بتلاوة الشيخ محمد اللحيدان: ١١٤ سورة و٧٧٬٤٢٩ كلمة، بصيغة quran.com ومكتبة QUL، لتظليل الكلمة لحظة نطقها في تطبيقات القرآن، وتكرار المقاطع للحفظ، والانتقال إلى أي كلمة. التكرارات التي يعيدها الشيخ في الصلاة موقَّتة بالترتيب الذي قيلت به.
